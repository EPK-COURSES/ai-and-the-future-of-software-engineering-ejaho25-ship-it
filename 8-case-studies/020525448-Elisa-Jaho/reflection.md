# Final Reflection

After completing this research, my opinion about AI in software engineering has changed. Before this assignment, I thought AI was mainly a tool for generating code. However, I learned that companies such as Microsoft, Google, Shopify, and Duolingo use AI in many different ways, including productivity improvement, workflow automation, testing, and content generation.

What surprised me most was how widely AI is already integrated into real software engineering environments. At the same time, I realized that AI cannot completely replace software engineers because human judgment, creativity, and responsibility are still necessary.

One concern I have is that developers may become too dependent on AI tools and stop improving their own problem-solving skills. However, I also see many opportunities because AI can reduce repetitive work and allow engineers to focus on more complex tasks.

In the future, I believe successful software engineers will be those who know how to work effectively with AI while still maintaining strong technical knowledge and critical thinking skills. Personally, I plan to learn how to use AI tools responsibly while continuing to improve my programming and software engineering fundamentals.
